# Marrskor product notes

Owners free. Nationwide. Perfect SKOR = 100.

## Scoring buckets (defaults)

- BASE 40
- Maintenance +45
- Repair documentation +10
- Recency +5
- Penalties subtract (missed items, odometer gaps)
- Stars use last-12-month star points; repair points count toward stars

## Ownership

1. Capture VIN (type or photo). Decode with NHTSA vPIC for specs only.
2. Registration or title photo. Mask street address before storage.
3. Optional: state digital vehicle credential (CA DMV Wallet first).
4. Optional: network shop confirms VIN in the bay.
5. Do not call a DMV owner-name API. DPPA blocks that for this app.

## Shop billing

- Network $19.95/mo
- Lead $49.95/mo — reminders routed to that shop + appointment option

Maintenance and Repair Records = red. SKOR = blue.
